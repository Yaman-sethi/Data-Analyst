I analysed about 10,800 Google Play Store apps in Python, cleaning the messy text columns (size, installs, price, dates), removing duplicates, and building new features like log installs and days since update.
I then used charts and a statistical test (Mann-Whitney) to answer questions on popular categories, most-installed apps, Free vs Paid, and what drives installs.
Key findings: 
Family has the most apps but Game has the most installs, installs and reviews are almost perfectly linked (correlation about 0.96), and rating alone barely explains popularity.
